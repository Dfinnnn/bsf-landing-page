The BSF model

Three parts that remove the biggest barriers to starting a farm.

  • Land access

    Start without buying land.

    Growers farm on land managed by Blue Sky Farms, so the biggest upfront cost is already handled.

  • Physical AI robots

    Less manual work in the field.

    Our robots are built to take on repetitive field work — spraying, crop checks and monitoring — alongside your team.

  • Grant support

    Lower your setup cost.

    We help growers find and apply for Malaysian government agricultural grants.

Land × AI × Grants = farming built to scale.

Our AI solution

A physical AI robot, crop-health detection and a farm dashboard, designed to work together.

  • Illustration

    Automate

    Field work, mechanised.

    A wheeled physical AI robot that carries its own tank and pump down the crop rows, misting plants on both sides as it moves. Built to run the same pass consistently, row after row.

  • Detect

    Crop health, flagged early.

    A vision system built to inspect plants along each row and flag early signs of pests or disease for a closer look. It is designed to support your team's judgement, not replace it.

Land access

Land is the biggest barrier to farming. We start there.

Blue Sky Farms manages more than 50 agricultural and industrial land plots across Malaysia, owned and through joint ventures. Growers in our programme farm on this land instead of buying their own, and landowners can partner with us to develop theirs.
